40% of the index is Chinese-language postings

OpenJob · November 19, 2026

Board

The OpenJob index is two indexes in one. Of the 5,107 job titles in our sample, 2,053 contain Chinese characters — 40.2%. Nearly a third of employer names do too (30.3%). The split runs exactly along the ATS pipes: 97.8% of Moka titles are Chinese-language, while Ashby and Greenhouse titles are 0% Chinese. Moka is the Chinese index; Ashby and Greenhouse are the English one; the same schema holds both.

China is the largest single geography at about a third of the index; the US is second at just over a quarter. The largest raw locations are 上海市, 中国 (477 rows), 深圳市, 广东, 中国 (430), 北京市, 中国 (301) — and "Hybrid" (374), which is a location value, not a place.

The fresh jobs tilt American

The fresh subset — the 2,245 rows posted in the last 60 days — tells a different story from the whole index. There, the US leads 30.6% to China's 28.0%. The reason is the pipes: Moka is the stalest source in the index (only 33.4% of its rows are fresh, against 67.2% for Ashby), so the Chinese half of the index ages faster than the English half. The archive is Chinese-heavy; the live hiring is more American than the headline suggests.

Why it matters

No other English-language job index ships 40% Chinese-language postings under the same schema. That is a genuine differentiator, not a marketing line: a Chinese-speaking job seeker's agent can search the same board, with the same fields, as an English-speaking one — and the agent roles are there in Chinese too. StepFun is hiring an "Agent Harness 技术leader" and an "AI Agent产品经理"; Weibo is hiring an "AI Agent研发工程师"; DJI lists "中/高级多媒体算法工程师（AI/AIGC/AI Agent）".

It also matters for employers. The hiring managers behind the Moka half of the index are reading a board that speaks their language — and OpenJob's native posts accept Chinese as well as English. If the index is going to grow, a good share of that growth will come from the side of it that the English-language launch never reaches.

How we counted

  • Source: the public manifest at https://openjob.softprobe.ai/api/data/manifest.json, generated 2026-11-18T20:08:03Z; 33,107 jobs, 34 shards, complete: true.
  • Sample: 5,107 rows from shards 0, 7, 14, 20, 27, 33. The shards are source-blocked, not random — each shard holds exactly one ATS system — so this is a cluster sample. The sample has no Lever rows (Lever is 6.0% of the full dataset) and overweights Ashby. Percentages are sample percentages.
  • Method: a title "contains CJK" when it contains at least one Chinese/Japanese/Korean character. Geography is a classifier over the free-text location field (China = contains 中国 or a Chinese city name; US = "United States"/"USA"/state code/US city; UK/Europe = named cities); 9.3% unclassified, 7.0% missing.
  • Indexed ≠ recruited. Every number describes rows indexed from employer ATS boards. OpenJob did not recruit these employers; the employers own the text.

The board is at openjob.softprobe.ai; the source is github.com/softprobe/openjob. A Chinese version of this story ships the same day.
