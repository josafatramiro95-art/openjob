AI agent jobs are 1.5% of the index — at Cloudflare, Stripe, Scale AI and Reddit

OpenJob · November 19, 2026

Board

Everyone is hiring for AI agents. Almost nobody is, if you count. We read all 5,107 job titles in our sample of the OpenJob index and matched them against a keyword set for agent work. Seventy-six titles matched. That is 1.5% of the index.

The small number is the honest one — and it is also the interesting one, because the 76 roles sit at exactly the companies the audience cares about, in English and in Chinese.

What counts as an agent role

We matched on the job title only (the title and the role field are identical on 100% of sample rows), word-boundary matched, with two sets:

  • Narrow "AI agent" set: agent, agents, agentic, llm, llms, genai, generative ai, prompt engineer, copilot, ai agent.
  • Broad "AI/ML" set: the narrow set plus ai, artificial intelligence, machine learning, ml, deep learning, nlp, natural language, computer vision, data scientist, data science, generative.

By source, Greenhouse boards carry the most agent roles (42 of 2,000 rows, 2.1%, across 10 employers), then Ashby (15 of 1,000, 1.5%, 5 employers), then Moka (17 of 2,100, 0.8%, 5 employers).

Small but real

1.5% is small enough to be a corrective to the hype and large enough to matter: 76 roles, at named employers, is a real job market, and it is the job market OpenJob itself is for. The board exists because the people who build agents do not browse job boards — they ask the agent they already use to find them a job. The roles above are what that agent should be finding.

How we counted

  • Source: the public manifest at https://openjob.softprobe.ai/api/data/manifest.json, generated 2026-11-18T20:08:03Z; 33,107 jobs, 34 shards, complete: true.
  • Sample: 5,107 rows from shards 0, 7, 14, 20, 27, 33. The shards are source-blocked, not random — each shard holds exactly one ATS system — so this is a cluster sample. The sample has no Lever rows (Lever is 6.0% of the full dataset) and overweights Ashby. Percentages are sample percentages.
  • Method: word-boundary keyword match on the job title, using the two sets above. Titles were not hand-classified; anything the keywords miss is missed, and anything they catch is counted.
  • Indexed ≠ recruited. Every number describes rows indexed from employer ATS boards. OpenJob did not recruit these employers; the employers own the text.
