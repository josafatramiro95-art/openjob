Half the index is 10 employers

OpenJob · November 19, 2026

Board

OpenJob's index holds 33,107 job rows from 81 employers. That is the shape of the whole dataset in one sentence: a small number of employers, covered very deeply. The top 5 employers supply 32.7% of the rows in our sample. The top 10 supply 48.2% — nearly half the index from ten companies.

Cloudflare alone is nearly one row in ten. The next 71 employers in the sample split the other half between them.

The concentration looks different per source

The index is fed by four ATS pipes, and each pipe has its own leaders. On Greenhouse boards the top employers are Cloudflare (493), Stripe (222), Robinhood (166), Scale AI (166) and Reddit (130). On Moka boards they are DJI (425), 远景能源 (312), 新浪微博 (220), 阶跃星辰 (220) and 赛智 SAJ (109). On Ashby boards the largest are far smaller — Antares (84), 1Password (76), Arrive (72) — because Ashby supplies only 12.1% of the full dataset, spread across more employers.

In other words, the index is really two indexes with different shapes: a concentrated English-language one and a concentrated Chinese-language one, each dominated by a handful of names.

Why this is a problem — and the fix

Concentration is not a flaw in the data; it is a fact about it. But it is a flaw in the board. A job seeker whose agent searches the index is mostly searching ten companies' careers pages. A hiring manager at an eleventh company is invisible. The index needs more employers, not more rows from the same ten — which is why OpenJob's target is 30 native employers posting directly, and why the native board exists at all: a post there is a Markdown file, one pull request, validated and auto-merged, live for every agent that reads the board.

If you hire at a company that is not one of these ten, your role is the one the index is missing. Post it: the walkthrough is in CONTRIBUTING.md under "Post a job" at github.com/softprobe/openjob.

How we counted

  • Source: the public manifest at https://openjob.softprobe.ai/api/data/manifest.json, generated 2026-11-18T20:08:03Z; 33,107 jobs, 34 shards, complete: true.
  • Sample: 5,107 rows from shards 0, 7, 14, 20, 27, 33. The shards are source-blocked, not random — each shard holds exactly one ATS system — so this is a cluster sample. The sample has no Lever rows (Lever is 6.0% of the full dataset) and overweights Ashby. Counts are sample counts; the full-dataset mix is Moka 42.6%, Greenhouse 39.3%, Ashby 12.1%, Lever 6.0%.
  • Indexed ≠ recruited. Every number describes rows indexed from employer ATS boards. OpenJob did not recruit these employers; the employers own the text.

The board is at openjob.softprobe.ai; the source is github.com/softprobe/openjob.
