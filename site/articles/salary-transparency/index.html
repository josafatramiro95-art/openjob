Only 1 in 16 jobs shows pay — and every one comes from Ashby

OpenJob · November 19, 2026

Board

Pay transparency in the OpenJob index is not a market-wide phenomenon. It is an Ashby phenomenon. Of the 5,107 rows in our sample, 318 carry a salary string — 6.2%, about one in sixteen. Every single one of them comes from an Ashby board. Greenhouse and Moka boards in the sample show pay on zero rows.

The numbers

Rows with a salary string
Whole sample318 of 5,107 — 6.2%
Ashby rows318 of 1,000 — 31.8%
Greenhouse rows0 of 2,000 — 0%
Moka rows0 of 2,100 — 0%

The structured salary field is null on 100% of sample rows — pay only ever appears as free text. Of the 318 rows with a salary string, 257 carry a parseable "$XK – $YK" range. Their median posted range is $150,000 – $205,000. The full spread runs from $22,000 to $500,000.

The employers that show pay most often are Antares (70 rows), Astera Institute (39), Assort Health (36), Agentio (27), Almedia (27), AeroVect (25) and AnySignal (23). The ranges themselves are ordinary and specific: "Senior Covenant Lawyer – Distressed" at $256K–$340K; "Sales Director, Buy-side" at $190K–$226K; "Customer Success Manager" at $90K–$105K.

What the median does and doesn't mean

The $150K–$205K median describes Ashby-posted roles only. It is not "what the index pays" and it is certainly not "what the market pays" — it is what the one ATS in the index that publishes salary text publishes, for the employers on it. Any story that quotes the median without that sentence is misquoting it, including this one if you stop reading here.

That caveat is the story. Pay transparency is a property of the pipe a job travels through, not of the job. A candidate whose agent searches the index will find pay on roughly a third of Ashby roles and on none of the Greenhouse or Moka roles — not because those employers hide pay more, but because their boards never carry the field. OpenJob's schema has a structured salary field for exactly this reason; it is null on every indexed row we sampled, because no source fills it.

How we counted

  • Source: the public manifest at https://openjob.softprobe.ai/api/data/manifest.json, generated 2026-11-18T20:08:03Z; 33,107 jobs, 34 shards, complete: true.
  • Sample: 5,107 rows from shards 0, 7, 14, 20, 27, 33. The shards are source-blocked, not random — each shard holds exactly one ATS system — so this is a cluster sample. The sample has no Lever rows (Lever is 6.0% of the full dataset) and overweights Ashby (19.6% of the sample vs 12.1% of the full dataset). Percentages are sample percentages.
  • Method: a row "shows pay" when its salary_text field is non-empty. The median is computed over the 257 rows with a parseable "$XK – $YK" range, Ashby only.
  • Indexed ≠ recruited. Every number describes rows indexed from employer ATS boards. OpenJob did not recruit these employers; the employers own the text.

The board is at openjob.softprobe.ai; the source is github.com/softprobe/openjob.
