We counted our own index honestly: only ~14,000 of 33,107 jobs were posted in the last 60 days

OpenJob · November 19, 2026

Board

OpenJob's index holds 33,107 job rows. That is the number on the front page, and it is true — but it is not the number a job seeker should care about. We sampled 5,107 rows and checked the posted date on every one. Only 2,245 of them — 44.0% — were posted in the last 60 days.

The honest live-jobs number is about 14,000, not 33,107. We are publishing the breakdown anyway, because a job board that audits its own index in public is believable in a way a round-number boast never is.

What we counted

The manifest (generated November 18, 2026, 20:08 UTC; snapshot a404cce5…) lists 33,107 jobs across 34 shards. We downloaded six shards — indices 0, 7, 14, 20, 27 and 33 — 5,107 rows in total, and verified both SHA-256 checksums (gzip and raw) on every shard before computing anything. "Fresh" means the employer stated a posted date on or after September 19, 2026, sixty days before the snapshot.

Weighted by each source's share of the full dataset, the fresh share is 41.9% — about 13,900 of 33,107 rows. The oldest posted date in the sample is April 25, 2019.

Freshness depends on where the row came from

The gap between sources is the story. Ashby boards are the freshest in the index: two in three Ashby rows were posted in the last 60 days. Greenhouse is in the middle at 43.4%. Moka, which supplies 42.6% of the full dataset, is the stalest at 33.4% — one in three.

The index itself was built recently (rows were first seen between November 15 and 18, 2026), but the postings it points to are old. Indexing a board is not the same as the employer hiring today, and the posted date is the only honest signal we have for that.

Why we are telling you this

Two reasons. First, the number a job seeker sees should be the number of jobs they can actually apply to. Presenting 33,107 as live jobs would send people — and their agents — to listings that expired years ago. Second, the fix is structural, not cosmetic: native posts on OpenJob expire after 60 days, so the board can never accumulate this kind of archive. The indexed half of the dataset is the part that still needs that discipline, and the first step is saying the number out loud.

How we counted

  • Source: the public manifest at https://openjob.softprobe.ai/api/data/manifest.json, generated 2026-11-18T20:08:03Z; 33,107 jobs, 34 shards, complete: true.
  • Sample: 5,107 rows from shards 0, 7, 14, 20, 27, 33. The shards are source-blocked, not random — each shard holds exactly one ATS system — so this is a cluster sample, not a random one. The sample has no Lever rows (Lever is 6.0% of the full dataset) and overweights Ashby (19.6% of the sample vs 12.1% of the full dataset). Percentages are sample percentages unless marked ATS-weighted.
  • "Fresh" is defined by the posted date the employer states on its own board (present on 100% of sample rows). Some boards may carry stale dates for still-open roles; "fresh" is defined by that date, not by re-verification.
  • Indexed ≠ recruited. Every number describes rows indexed from employer ATS boards. OpenJob did not recruit these employers; the employers own the text.
